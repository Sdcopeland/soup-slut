Event Details

What

A legendary gathering for our community to share their best homemade soups. Bring a pot of your finest creation, taste what others have made, and vote for the ultimate Soup Champion.

When & Where

Date: Saturday, October 25th, 2025

Time: 6:00 PM - 9:00 PM

Location: The Community Hall, 123 Soup St, Denver, CO

The Rules

  • Soup must be homemade.
  • Bring your own serving ladle.
  • Label your soup with its name.
  • List major allergens clearly.
  • One vote per person.
  • Have fun!
