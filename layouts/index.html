Event Details

What

A legendary gathering for our community to share their best homemade soups and raise money for charity. Bring a pot of your finest creation, taste what others have made, and vote for the ultimate Soup Slut.

When & Where

Date: Saturday, November 7th, 2026

Time: 5:00 PM - 9:00 PM

Location: Location information provided on registration

The Rules

  • Soup must be homemade.
  • No prep at location, soup must be ready.
  • Bring your own serving ladle.
  • Try to bring a crockpot or warming device, sterno & hotel tray will be provided
  • List major allergens clearly and ingredients.
  • One vote per person.
  • Please donate to our charity sponsor, SECOR Cares if you can't make soup make a bigger donation!
